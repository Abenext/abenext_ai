### Abenext AI 1.3.0

**New: Collections Agent**

- Sends polite reminders to customers with overdue invoices: a friendly reminder, a second reminder and a final notice, each sent once and only when due.
- One message per customer lists all their overdue invoices in their own currency, with the invoice PDFs attached.
- Starts switched off and in Test Mode: messages go to your test address until you are ready. Open **AI Collections Settings** and use **Preview Today's Reminders** first.
- New fields on the Customer: Collections Email, Collections Mobile, Reminder Language, Stop Reminders and Promised Payment Date.
- With an Abenext AI plan, reminders are worded by AI in English or Roman Urdu. Amounts and invoice numbers always come from ERPNext.
