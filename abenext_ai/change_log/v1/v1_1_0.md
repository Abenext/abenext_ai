### Abenext AI 1.1.0

**New: Audit Watchdog**

- Checks your books every night and lists anything that needs attention: duplicate supplier invoices, negative stock, unusual Journal Entries, sudden cost changes and duplicate Stock Entries (drafts included).
- Open **Abenext AI → AI Watchdog Finding** to see the findings, open the documents and mark each one Fixed or Ignored.
- Use **Run Checks Now** to check immediately.
