### Abenext AI 1.4.0

**New: Audit Watchdog dashboard**

- Open **Abenext AI → Audit Watchdog** for one screen with every finding: audit areas with High and Medium counts on the left, findings as cards in the middle, and an assistant on the right.
- For each finding: why it matters, recommended actions, and how similar findings were resolved before.
- Mark findings Fixed or Ignored, run the checks and preview the morning report from the same screen.
- With an Abenext AI plan, **Explain with AI** gives a plain-language analysis of the selected finding.

**A cleaner Abenext AI workspace**

- "Your work today" shows the Audit Watchdog dashboard, statements waiting for review, open findings and payment reminders, with live counts.
- Each tool has its own card with plain names, including links to Bank Accounts and Customers.
- Abenext AI now has its own logo on the apps screen.
