### Abenext AI 1.2.0

**Audit Watchdog: 13 checks**

- Eight new checks: duplicate payments, the same bank account on two parties, duplicate sales invoices, negative cash balance, selling below cost, back-dated entries, old uncleared bank entries and customers over their credit limit.
- Findings about balances (stock, cash, bank, credit limits) now update every night and close themselves when fixed.

**Morning report**

- A daily summary of open findings by email and notification. Switch it on in **AI Watchdog Settings**, and try **Preview Report** first.
- With an Abenext AI plan, the report starts with a short plain-language summary and the first things to do.

**Bank reconciliation**

- New bank formats: Easypaisa, Faysal Bank, JazzCash and Raqami, alongside Meezan Bank.
- Reads statements that list the newest transaction first, have separate fee columns, or show one signed amount column.
- Sites with several companies can set the bank charges account for each company in **AI Reconciliation Settings**.
